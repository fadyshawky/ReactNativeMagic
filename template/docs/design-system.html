01 — Identity

The FS monogram

A forward-leaning "FS" mark — the italic stance signals motion and momentum. Four official variants cover dark, mono, light, and mark-only placements.

Primary
Gradient chip, ink strokes. Default everywhere.
Mono
#0D1124 chip, white strokes. Dark surfaces.
Light
#F4F6FE chip, ink strokes. Print & light UI.
Mark only
#2F6BFF strokes, no chip. Watermarks & favicons.

✓ Do
  • Preserve clear-space of at least half the chip width on every side.
  • Use the mono or light variant when the gradient lacks contrast.
  • Keep the chip's 30/120 corner radius ratio when scaling.
× Don't
  • Recolor the gradient or swap stop order (always blue_600 → blue_300).
  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark away from its lean.
  • Place the gradient chip on busy or low-contrast photography.
