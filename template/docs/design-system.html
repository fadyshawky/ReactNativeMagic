Design system

Principles

Flat, bordered, one accent

  • Every surface has a 1px border; the shadow only hints at layering.
  • One accent — electric blue — for the primary action, active nav, selected state and links. Nothing else is blue.
  • No gradients (brand marks aside), no glow, no pill buttons, no emoji, no Title Case.
  • Geist for everything; Geist Mono for eyebrows, amounts, IDs, counts and timestamps.
  • Light and dark carry equal weight. Dark mode steps surfaces up from the canvas.
Brand

Marks

An angular interlocked F and S on a 14° slant. <Logo variant="mark" /> follows the theme. Raw files in src/assets/brand/. Never re-space, re-colour or re-draw.
